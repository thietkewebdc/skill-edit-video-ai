# Style spec — the "FDown competitor" reel look

Reverse-engineered from `FDown.vn_Tai_video_Facebook_720p (HD)_94c0.mp4`
(Vietnamese AI/business creator, talking-head reel about using AI to edit video).

## Format & feel
- Vertical **9:16**, talking head, medium-close, high energy, lots of hand gestures.
- Fast pace: **jump-cuts** remove every pause/breath → no dead second.
- Warm, slightly saturated grade; subject well lit; clean or blurred background.

## Captions (the signature)
- **Karaoke, phrase-by-phrase**, one short chunk at a time, synced to speech.
- Two colors alternating by role:
  - **WHITE bold uppercase** = emphasis / punch words ("AI LÀM HẾT", "ĐỪNG LO", "QUAY VIDEO").
  - **YELLOW italic-ish** = connective / flow lines ("hướng dẫn cho bạn", "video như này").
- Big font for the punchiest words/numbers; heavy black outline so legible over anything.
- Bottom-center, roughly lower third.

## Pop-up label callouts
- Small rounded **pill** labels appear on emphasis, near the hand/gesture:
  - `GIỎI CÔNG NGHỆ ❌`, `GÕ TAY ❌`, `1 QUY TRÌNH ✅`, `QUAY VIDEO`.
- White text on a solid rounded pill; a red ❌ or green ✅ badge conveys bad/good.
  (Our build uses color to convey ❌/✅: red pill = bad/cost, green pill = good/saving.)

## B-roll / inserts
- Cuts away to **screen recordings** shown as a rounded-corner card with an **orange glowing
  border** on a **dark textured background** (grid/tech). Card doesn't fill the frame.
- Person often **shrinks** into a smaller framed window while the insert/graphic fills the rest.

## The "VIDEO GỐC vs AI ĐÃ EDIT" reveal
- Before/after: two phone-shaped cards side by side, a "VS" badge, edited one larger.

## Narrative / script formula (P-A-S + open loop)
1. **Hook 0–3s:** deny a common habit + poke pain, or promise a shocking result/number.
2. **Promise + remove objections:** "không cần giỏi công nghệ / không cần phần mềm".
3. **Demo = proof:** real screen recording of doing the thing.
4. **Re-hook mid-video:** "nhưng đây mới là điều quan trọng nhất" (anti-scroll).
5. **Social proof:** "kể cả video này cũng do AI làm".
6. **CTA comment-bait:** "bình luận chữ QUAN TÂM, mình gửi thông tin".

## Title/hook phrasing that works
MIỄN PHÍ · MỚI NHẤT 2026 · KIẾM TIỀN · 1 PROMPT · KHÔNG LỘ MẶT · TỰ ĐỘNG · A-Z ·
money numbers up front (e.g. "CẮT 100 TRIỆU/NĂM").

## Our adaptation for the user (Cường — business/AI/web niche)
- Same devices, but content = "AI cho chủ doanh nghiệp: cắt chi phí, tự động hoá, web/CRM".
- Money numbers are the strongest hook (he has real ones: 50tr, 100tr).
- Brand accent options: competitor orange `#FF9614`, or user's purple `#5946CE`.
- Always end with comment-bait CTA "BÌNH LUẬN: QUAN TÂM".
